Support

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game? Write to trungviet1506@gmail.com.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There is no account, so if you delete the app, your statistics are deleted too.

How do I change the ad settings?

Open Settings → Privacy and ad settings in the app (shown where the law requires it), or change the advertising settings of your phone.

A puzzle seems wrong.

Every puzzle is checked by a program to have exactly one solution. If you think something is wrong, please send us a screenshot.

About Sunday Sudoku

Version 1.0.0

Sunday Sudoku is calm, classic Sudoku with big, clear numbers. It is made to be easy on the eyes and pleasant to play every day.

What makes it different

• Five levels, from Beginner to Expert. Difficulty is measured by the solving techniques a person needs, not just by the number of clues.

• Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

• Hints explain why a number belongs in a square.

• A new daily challenge every day, with a calendar and a streak.

• Works without internet.

• Only one small banner ad. No pop-up ads, no videos, no sounds.
